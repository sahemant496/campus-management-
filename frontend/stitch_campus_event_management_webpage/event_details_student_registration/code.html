Participant Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What hardware or computers should I bring?

Bring your laptop, charger, and any preferred testing devices. Hardware kits (Raspberry Pi 5, Arduino bundles, LiDAR breakout boards) can be checked out for free with your student ID at the Lab Desk.

How do team sizes work? Can I register solo?

Teams may consist of 2 to 4 students. You are fully welcome to register as a 'Solo Hacker'—we host a designated team mixer during Day 1 check-in where 40% of attendees form teams.

Is catering, meals, and snacks included?

Yes, full catering is completely covered by University Student Affairs, including 7 meals, coffee stands, vegan/halal/gluten-free options, and late-night snacks.

Will this cover my academic attendance / lab absence?

Yes. Apex 2026 is an accredited University Flagship Activity. All registered students receive an official excused-absence certification signed by the Dean of Academic Affairs.
